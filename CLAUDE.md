# P32W

A 32-bit CPU in discrete TTL on 9U × 340 mm cards, and its operating system,
P32W Unix. Ken's design; P8X and YACC1-D are its relatives.

- The machine: docs/architecture.md; the bus: docs/bus.md (generated by
  generators/p32bus.py). Decisions are logged in docs/decisions.md (D1, D2,
  ...); record new decisions there as dated entries at the end.
- Use conventional commits (feat:, fix:, chore:).
- The ISA's single source of truth is microcode/p32isa.py; the ISA is
  docs/isa-v0.md. The assembler imports it; emulator/p32opc.h is generated
  from it (never edit the header).
- Build and test: `cd emulator && make test`. Run after any change to the ISA
  table, microcode, assembler, emulators, kernel or software.
- Related repos (reference only unless asked): ~/Developer/p8x, ~/Developer/YACC1-D.
